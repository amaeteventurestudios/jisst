Privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026

The short version: chats between people are end-to-end encrypted, so we cannot read them. We keep what the app needs to work (your account, your chats with agents, alerts) and nothing for advertising.

What we keep

  • Your account. Your email address (used to send your sign-in code), the name, @handle and photo you choose, and the people and groups you join.
  • Private chats with people. Messages, photos, voice notes and calls in these chats are end-to-end encrypted on your device. Our server stores and passes them on, but cannot read them. If you turn on Chat backup, your encrypted keys are stored with us and only your recovery key can open them.
  • Chats with agents. These are not end-to-end encrypted, because the agent has to read your message to answer. The app says so in every agent chat.
  • Settings and alerts. Your privacy switches (last seen, read ticks), and the address your browser gives us for message and call alerts.
  • Agents you make. If you make your own agent, its name, description and chat account. The agent's key is shown to you once; your agent reads only the chats it is added to.
  • Reports. If you report a message or a person, the report and the messages you include reach our safety team.
  • Server logs. Like any service, our servers briefly record requests (address, time, page) to keep Jisste running and safe.

What stays on your device

  • Your encryption keys, unless you turn on Chat backup.
  • The check that blurs explicit photos runs on your phone. Photos are not sent anywhere to be checked.

Who processes it

  • Jisste runs on our own server in the United States: the chat server, sign-in, calls, translation and alerts. Translation runs on that server too; your text is not sent to an outside translation service.
  • Signing in goes through the shared account used by Noveniqe's games and apps, so one account works across them.
  • Alerts on your phone are delivered by your browser's own push service (for example Apple's or Google's). They carry no message text from private chats.
  • Maps in shared places load from OpenStreetMap, which sees the usual request details, as any website does.

We do not sell any of this and do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Messages stay until you delete them, or until the timer runs out in chats with disappearing messages. Your account stays until you ask us to delete it. We keep a nightly backup of the server for a short time so we can recover from a fault.

Your choices

  • Turn last seen, online and read ticks on or off in You.
  • Delete messages, block people and sign linked computers out from your phone.
  • To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, write to support@noveniqe.com. We answer within 30 days.
  • If you live in the EU, the UK or another place with data protection law, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

You must be 13 or older to use Jisste (16 in EU countries that set a higher age). If you believe a child under 13 has an account, write to support@noveniqe.com and we will delete it.
